## 29/07/2026 [ Day 26: IoT Security ]

Today's focus: **Internet of Things (IoT) security** -  smart cameras, routers, thermostats, smart plugs, basically anything with a chip and a Wi-Fi connection that doesn't quite count as a "real" computer but is absolutely connected to the internet anyway.

- **Common IoT vulnerabilities:** default or weak passwords that owners never bother changing (the single biggest one, by a wide margin), outdated firmware that rarely if ever gets patched, and unnecessary services left exposed directly to the internet (remember Shodan from Day 24 -  this is precisely the kind of thing it indexes by the millions).
- **IoT penetration testing** follows the same general process as everything else this month, but frequently includes physically examining the device too -  checking exposed debug ports, pulling and analyzing the firmware directly off the chip.

The running theme of the entire day: IoT devices are, overwhelmingly, built for cheapness and convenience first, with security treated as a distant, often entirely skipped, third priority.
